## Offset (Previous/Next) AND Positional Functions (First/Last Value)

Functions:

- `FIRST_VALUE(expr)`
- `LAST_VALUE(expr)`
- `LAG(expr, offset, default)`
- `LEAD(expr, offset, default)`
- `NTH_VALUE(expr, n)`

**Note:** Without explicit frame, `LAST_VALUE` often returns just the current row.
